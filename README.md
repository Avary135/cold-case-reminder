# cold-case-reminder
1. No Data Collection
This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not have a server, and we do not track your name, email address, IP address, or location.

2. Browsing Privacy
We use the activeTab permission. This means the extension only "wakes up" when you interact with it.

It cannot see your browsing history.

It cannot see what you are doing on other websites.

It cannot see your passwords or private data.

3. Anonymity in Reporting
This extension is a bridge, not a database. When you click "Report a Tip Anonymously," you are directed to the official Metro Denver Crime Stoppers website.

All tips are handled by their encrypted, third-party reporting software.

This extension does not see the tips you submit or any information you provide to law enforcement.

4. Local Logic
The "Cold Case of the Day" is chosen locally on your computer based on the date. No information about which cases you view is ever sent back to us or to any third party.
